Passage (original practice material): Cities are planting more trees, and the reasons go beyond beauty. A mature street tree can lower the air temperature around it by several degrees through shade and evaporation, which reduces the energy homes need for cooling. Studies in several countries have linked greener neighbourhoods with lower rates of anxiety, partly because green spaces encourage walking and casual social contact. However, trees are not a cheap solution. Young trees need watering for their first three years, and in dry cities up to a third may die before they are established. Roots can also damage pavements if the wrong species is planted in a narrow street. For these reasons, researchers argue that planting programmes succeed only when maintenance budgets are protected for at least a decade — a condition that election cycles rarely guarantee.
